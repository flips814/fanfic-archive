Add works

Paste links to stories and they'll be imported in the background. You'll follow them automatically.

You can paste several at once. Progress shows on the Activity page.

Supported sites

  • AO3

    Work links like /works/123456. For works locked to logged-in users, add your AO3 login in Settings.

  • FanFiction.net

    Story links like /s/1234567/1/. Also FictionPress. Pages load slowly because of Cloudflare protection.

  • Wattpad

    Story links like /story/123456789-title.

  • Tumblr

    A single post (one chapter), or a blog's tag page, where each post becomes a chapter and new posts become new chapters. Blogs that need a Tumblr login can't be imported.

  • And more

    Royal Road, SpaceBattles, Sufficient Velocity and every other site FanFicFare supports (opens in a new tab).
